We retain personal data for as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, all personal data (profile, events, clubs, messages, booking history) is permanently deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (e.g. accounting and tax records are retained for 10 years in accordance with Slovak law).

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights with respect to your personal data:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18) — limit how we use your data in certain circumstances
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Right to withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior processing

You can exercise most of these rights directly in the app under Settings. For other requests, contact us at admin@woeva.com. We will respond within 30 days.
